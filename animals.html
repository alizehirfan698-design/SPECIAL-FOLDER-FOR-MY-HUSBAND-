If the universe had to put Alizeh and Omer into animals, it would take its time — because some souls are too specific to rush. Every creature below holds a little piece of us, a little piece of what we are to each other. Soft in the right places, fierce when it counts, and always, always finding our way back. 🌙

Alizeh & Omer — together

A deer and a wolf shouldn't make sense. But Alizeh is the kind of gentle that makes even the wildest thing go calm, and Omer is the kind of fierce that makes even the softest thing feel completely safe. Together they're not opposites — they're the most natural thing in the world. Two different creatures who found the same home in each other. 🌙
